Passport Size Photo Maker Pakistan

How to use the Passport Photo Maker

A passport-size photograph is often needed for educational admissions, job applications and official documents in Pakistan. Different organizations can require different photo dimensions, so always check their instructions before submitting your photograph.

Choose a clear photograph from your device. A well-lit photo with your face clearly visible usually gives better results. Select the white canvas option if you want a white canvas behind the image, then click Create Passport Photo. The tool creates a 35 × 45 mm image at 300 DPI and a printable sheet containing four copies.

The white canvas option does not automatically remove the original background. Review the finished photograph before using it for an official application. Keep your original photo so you can make another version if needed.

Processing happens in your browser. You do not need an account or paid subscription. The output is a JPG image that you can save to your device.

35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI.

Check the specific dimensions required by the employer.

No, the white option creates a white canvas only.

Percentage Calculator Pakistan

How to Calculate Your Marks Percentage

Students in Pakistan often need to calculate their Matric, Intermediate and university marks percentage. A percentage shows how many marks you obtained compared with the total marks available in an examination.

Enter your obtained marks in the first field and total marks in the second field. Press the calculate button to see your percentage. The formula is obtained marks divided by total marks, multiplied by 100.

For example, 850 marks out of 1100 gives approximately 77.27%. This calculation can help you understand your result and compare it with admission requirements.

Grade boundaries differ between educational boards and institutions. The grade displayed by this calculator is an estimate, not an official board grade or admission decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is percentage calculated?

Divide obtained marks by total marks and multiply by 100.

Can I calculate Matric percentage?

Yes, enter your Matric marks.

Can I calculate Intermediate percentage?

Yes, the same formula works.

Are grades official?

No, grade boundaries vary.

Age Calculator

How to Calculate Your Exact Age

An age calculator helps you find your current age using your date of birth. It can be useful for school forms, job applications, registration forms and other documents that ask for your age.

Select your date of birth and click Calculate Age. The tool displays your age in years, months and days. It also calculates the time remaining until your next birthday.

Always enter the correct date of birth. For official applications, use the date shown on your relevant identity or educational document. The calculation uses your device's current date.

The tool works directly in your browser. You do not need to register or send your date of birth to an external service.

Image Resizer for Government Job Forms

How to Resize Images for Government Job Forms

Many online job application forms specify a maximum image file size. A photograph that is too large may not upload successfully. An image resizer can help create a smaller JPG image for online submission.

Select a photograph and choose a target size such as 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB. Click the resize button to generate a compressed JPG image.

Compression can affect picture quality. Preview the result and make sure important details remain clear. The resulting file size is an estimate and may differ depending on the original image.

Different recruitment websites may require different dimensions, formats and file sizes. Read the official application instructions before uploading your photograph.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize to 50 KB?

Yes, select 50 KB.

Compression can reduce quality.

JPG format.

No, the target is an estimate.
